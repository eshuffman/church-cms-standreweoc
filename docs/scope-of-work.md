# Overall Goal

Create a modern church website for St. Andrew EOC that looks clean and minimalistic. The site will include the following core pages:

- [Home page](#home-page)
- [About page](#about-page)
- [Visitors page](#visitors-page)
- [Calendar of services](#calendar-page)
- [Contact page](#contact-page)
    - [Hall rental page](#hall-rental-information)
- [Fish fry schedule](#fish-fry-schedule)
    - [Volunteer Info](#volunteer-info)

And include the following features:
- [Donate button](#donate-button)
- [Social media links](#social-media-links)

Additionally, the site integrate the ChurchCMS platform for administrative work, to include:

- Member Profiles
- Searchable directory
- Online tithing
- Giving statements
- Church school registration

**ChurchCMS will be its own thang, accessible from our new domain via an admin link. The two sites will not interact; our public-facing site will not be built using ChurchCMS.**

## Scope of Work

### Tangibles

**Go Live Date:** Phil wants budgeting portion/donations by October 1. (New fiscal year) Jarrod wants it by November 1.
**Budget:** Ideally $300/year or less, with the threshold of $360.

### Core Features

#### Home Page

As a visitor, I want to feel welcomed to the church through the website, and be able to easily navigate to pages I may be interested in. I don't want to have to scroll far or through old articles or photos to find what I'm looking for.

- Short welcome text
- Link to each page
- Footer
    - Address
    - Phone number
    - Link to donation page
    - Social media icon/s

#### About Page

As a visitor, I'd like to find out more about the history of St. Andrew and who the priest in charge is.

- Short description of the history of St. Andrew's
- Photos of Metropolitan, Bishop Daniel, Fr. Jarrod

#### Visitors Page

As a visitor, I want to find out more about Orthodoxy and what it's like to visit St. Andrew.

- Links to online resources
- FAQ on Orthodoxy, our church

#### Calendar Page

As a parishioner, I want an easy way to see when services will be held.

- Integrated Google calendar
- Button to subscribe to calendar

#### Contact Page

As a visitor, I'd like to be able to contact the priest and figure out how to get to the parish.

- Contact form to fill out
    - Form sends email to Jarrod
- Map of location
- Address, phone number
- Social links

#### Hall Rental Information

As a visitor, I want information about renting the hall -- how much it costs, when it can be rented, general rules, who to contact to proceed.

#### Fish Fry Schedule

As a visitor, I want to know when fish fries are held. 

#### Volunteer info

As a parishioner, I want to know how to get involved in serving at the fish fries and making pierogi, including dates for each event and what to expect.

#### Donate Button

As a parishioner, I want an easy, convenient way to give to the church without having to remember to bring my checkbook to church. 

#### Social Media Links

As a visitor, I'd like to see updates of what the church has been doing recently. The best place to go is the church's Facebook page, which is updated on a weekly basis.
