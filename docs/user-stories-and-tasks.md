# User Stories

 - Charitable Giving: As a user, I want to have an easy and convenient way to donate to the church, so that I don't have to worry about remembering to bring my checkbook to church on Sunday or remember how to send a letter.

- Payment Management: As the church finance guy, I want to have an easy way to track donations so that I don't have to double-enter donations into Quickbooks on my laptop. **Ideally this happens before the new fiscal year on October 1.**

- Registration Management: As a church school administrator, I want to have an easy way to receive registrations for church school and decide who will go into each class.

- Member Directory: As a church member, I want to have an easy way to recognize who the other members of the church are so I can connect better with them at coffee hour.
